Retold Support

Retold · Life stories, spoken, kept on your iPhone

Retold records the memories you talk about, writes down exactly what you said, and keeps each one with its recording. Everything stays on your iPhone.

Need help? Email us at support@martinapps.dev and we'll respond within one business day.

Getting Started

  • Tap Record (or press the Action button, if you've set it up) and talk about a memory.
  • Tap Stop when you're done. Retold writes down what you said on your iPhone.
  • On the filing screen, confirm the title, people and places Retold suggests, or change them. Nothing is filed until you confirm.
  • Browse your stories by period of life, by person, or by place. Each story shows its transcript and plays its recording.

Frequently Asked Questions

The microphone or speech recognition doesn't work.

Open Settings → Retold and turn on Microphone and Speech Recognition, then return to Retold.

Where are my recordings and transcripts stored?

Only on your iPhone, inside Retold. Retold has no account and no servers. If you have iCloud or computer backups turned on, they are included in your iPhone's own backup.

How do I use the Action button?

Open Settings → Action Button → Controls and choose the Retold control. Pressing the Action button then opens Retold ready to record.

I don't see suggested questions.

Suggested questions use Apple Intelligence on supported iPhones. On other iPhones, recording, filing and browsing work the same, just without suggestions.

How do I keep my stories somewhere else?

Use Export to save everything (every recording, every transcript and an index) as a single zip, then choose where to send it from the share sheet.

Can I delete a recording?

You can delete a recording that hasn't been filed yet from inside the app. Deleting the app deletes everything Retold stored.
