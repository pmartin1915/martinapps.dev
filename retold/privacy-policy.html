Privacy Policy

Retold for iOS · Last updated October 19, 2026

Overview

Retold helps you keep the stories of your life in your own voice. It records what you say, writes down the words on your iPhone, and keeps each memory with its recording. Retold collects no personal data. Everything stays on your iPhone unless you choose to export it.

In short: no account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking, and no servers. Retold makes no network requests.

Data Collection

Retold does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to us or to anyone else. Specifically:

  • No analytics or telemetry
  • No tracking of any kind
  • No advertising
  • No user accounts or sign-up
  • No data is sent to any server operated by us or by a third party

Microphone and Speech Recognition

Retold uses the microphone only while you are recording. Speech recognition, which turns your recording into text, runs on your iPhone. You can turn either permission off at any time in Settings → Retold.

Data Stored on Your iPhone

Retold keeps the following inside its own app storage on your iPhone, protected by iOS Data Protection:

  • Recordings: the audio of each memory you record
  • Transcripts: the words you said, as written down on your iPhone
  • What you confirm: the titles, people, places, periods of life and follow-up questions you choose to file

Suggestions for titles, people, places and questions use Apple Intelligence on your iPhone when it is available. They are produced on the device.

You can delete a recording that has not been filed. Deleting the app deletes everything Retold stored.

Backups

Your library is included in your iPhone's own backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup), if you have backups turned on. Those backups are managed by Apple and by you; Retold has no access to them.

Exporting

Nothing leaves Retold unless you export it. When you export your library, Retold makes a single zip of every recording, every transcript and an index. You choose where it goes from the iOS share sheet. What happens to the file after that is up to you and the service you share it with. Recordings may mention other people, so share with care.

Third-Party Services

Retold includes no third-party SDKs and does not send data to or receive data from any third-party service, API, or server.

Children's Privacy

Retold is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes to This Policy

If this ever changes (for example, if a later version adds sync), this policy will be updated before that version ships, with a new date on this page.
