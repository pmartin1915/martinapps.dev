About Martin Apps

We build small, careful tools and say plainly what each one can and can't do.

What we believe

An app should do one job well. It should say where its information comes from, work when the signal drops, and keep your data on your device. No ads, and we never sell data.

Founder

Perry Martin, DNP, RN, FNP-C, is a board-certified family nurse practitioner with a background in pediatric critical care. He founded Martin Apps LLC, writes the code himself, and directs every product's content.
