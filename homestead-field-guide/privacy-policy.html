Homestead Field Guide Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

Overview

Homestead Field Guide is an offline reference application for planning and running a backyard food garden and a small homestead. Its guides and calculators are built into the app and work with no connection.

No accounts, no servers, no tracking: The app has no backend. Nothing you enter or view is collected by us or uploaded anywhere.

Data Collection

We do NOT collect, receive, transmit, or share:

  • Personal information
  • Your location
  • Camera images, photos, or video
  • Device identifiers
  • Usage analytics or crash telemetry
  • Browsing history

A few settings are saved on your own device so the app remembers them — see On-Device Storage below. That data stays on your device and is never uploaded.

Nothing is sent automatically: All content lookup and all calculations happen on your device. The app opens a web page or the App Store only when you tap a link that says so (for example, another app from Martin Apps LLC); that page then opens in your browser or the App Store, outside this app. If you use the web version rather than the iPhone app, your browser downloads and updates the app's own files from the site hosting it.

On-Device Storage

The application saves the following on your device, so your settings persist between sessions:

  • Theme preference (4 display modes) and the larger-text setting
  • Unit system preference (imperial/metric)
  • Guides you bookmark
  • Whether you have dismissed the welcome tips

This data stays on your device and is never sent to us or to anyone else. The one place a copy can go is your own device backup: on iPhone, if iCloud Backup is turned on, iOS includes this data in that backup, as it does for most apps. That backup belongs to your Apple account and we have no access to it. The Android app opts out of both cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. You can erase all of it at any time by deleting the app.

Permissions

The app requests no device permissions. It does not use:

  • Location
  • Camera
  • Microphone
  • Motion & orientation sensors
  • Contacts
  • Photo library
  • Apple Health / HealthKit
  • Push notifications

Third-Party Services

Homestead Field Guide does not use any third-party services. There are no analytics, advertising, tracking, or data-sharing integrations of any kind, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

Children's Privacy

This is a general-audience educational app. We do not collect any information from any user, including children under 13. There is no data collection mechanism.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the application after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
