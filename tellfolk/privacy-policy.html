Privacy Policy

Tellfolk for iOS · Last updated October 19, 2026

Overview

Tellfolk helps you record a conversation with someone you care about. It shows one question at a time from a question deck and records the answer with your iPhone's camera and microphone. Tellfolk collects no personal data. Your recordings stay on your iPhone unless you choose to export them.

In short: no account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking, and no servers. Tellfolk makes no network requests.

Data Collection

Tellfolk does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to us or to anyone else. Specifically:

  • No analytics or telemetry
  • No tracking of any kind
  • No advertising
  • No user accounts or sign-up
  • No data is sent to any server operated by us or by a third party

Camera and Microphone

Tellfolk asks for camera and microphone access so it can record video and sound of the person you are interviewing. It uses them only while the recorder is open. You can turn either permission off at any time in Settings → Tellfolk.

Data Stored on Your iPhone

Tellfolk keeps the following inside its own app storage on your iPhone:

  • Recordings: the video files of each answer you record
  • Session list: for each session, which question deck you used, when you started, which questions were answered, and which clips belong to each answer
  • A recovery log: a small file that lets Tellfolk find a recording again if the app closes unexpectedly while recording

Tellfolk also reads the size and creation date of its own files and the free space on your iPhone, to show how much space recordings use and to warn you when your iPhone is almost full. This information stays on your iPhone.

Deleting a session in Tellfolk deletes its recordings. Deleting the app deletes everything Tellfolk stored.

Backups

Recordings are included in your iPhone's own backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup), if you have backups turned on, so you can get them back on a new phone. Those backups are managed by Apple and by you; Tellfolk has no access to them.

Exporting

Nothing leaves Tellfolk unless you export it. When you export a session (as one video, or one video per answer):

  • Save to Photos: Tellfolk asks for permission to add to your photo library. It cannot read, change, or delete anything already in your library.
  • Save or share: you choose the destination in the iOS share sheet. Once a file is shared, the app or service you sent it to handles it under its own privacy policy.

The People You Record

Please make sure everyone on camera agrees to be recorded. Tellfolk shows a consent card before each session to help with that. You decide what to keep, delete, and share.

Third-Party Services

Tellfolk includes no third-party SDKs and does not send data to or receive data from any third-party service, API, or server.

Children's Privacy

Tellfolk does not collect data from any user, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
