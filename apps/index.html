Apps

Five apps, each built to do one job well. Grouped by what they help with.

Study and clinical learning

BoardBound

iPhoneFree to start

Study for the FNP board exam with an AI tutor that explains the reasoning behind each answer.

Good to know: questions are AI-written and machine-checked, not reviewed by a clinician.

Learn more

Burn Wizard

iPhoneFree

Learn how burn size and fluid needs in children are worked out, step by step, offline.

Good to know: for learning only. It is not a medical device and not for patient care.

Learn more

Outdoors

Wilderness Survival Companion

iPhoneAndroidFree

Survival know-how and field tools for hikers, hunters and rescue volunteers. Every entry names its source.

Good to know: works with no signal at all. No account, no tracking, no ads.

Learn more

Focus and screen time

Shortless

iPhone and iPadFree

Hide Shorts, Reels and TikTok feeds in Safari, and keep the rest of each site working.

Good to know: works in Safari only. Apple doesn't let blockers change other browsers or apps.

Learn more

Scrollometer

iPhoneFree

See your screen time as a distance: feet, then miles, then landmarks.

Good to know: distances are estimates, and the app shows the math behind every number.
