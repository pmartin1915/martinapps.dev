Shortless

Hide YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, TikTok and Snapchat Spotlight in Safari, and keep the rest of each site working.

Get help

iPhone and iPad · Free

See it in action

What it does

Pick what to hide

A separate switch for each feed. Hide one, or all four.

See the time you got back

A Time Reclaimed dashboard, a scroll-free streak, and home and lock screen widgets.

Set a goal

Say how much you watch now and pick a target, from 25% less to none.

Before you download

  • Works in Safari only. iOS doesn't let any app change the TikTok, Instagram, YouTube or Snapchat apps.
  • Regular YouTube videos, Instagram photos and Snapchat messages keep working.
  • No account, no analytics and no servers. Everything stays on your device.
