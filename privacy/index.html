Privacy

No ads, and we never sell your data. Most of our apps collect nothing at all. When one has to send something, its policy says what, where and why.

App by app

BoardBound

Needs an account, so your study progress can be kept on our servers and follow you. The AI tutor sends a question to Anthropic only after you agree. Crash reports and subscriptions use outside services named in the policy. You can delete your account and its data at any time.
