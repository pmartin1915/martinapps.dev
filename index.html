About

Martin Apps LLC is an Alabama limited liability company building education software for nurses and nurse practitioners. Our products are designed by active clinicians and students, validated against the actual exam blueprints and clinical workflows they're meant to support, and held to safety and accuracy standards appropriate for the healthcare context.

We focus on a narrow, deep niche — FNP board certification preparation today, with adjacent NP specialties on the roadmap — rather than competing with general-purpose study apps. Our founder, Perry Martin, is an active DNP-FNP candidate at the University of Alabama at Birmingham, with a clinical background in pediatric critical care and neuro-oncology.
