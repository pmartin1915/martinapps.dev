Wilderness Survival Companion

Offline reference and field tools for the backcountry.

This 100% offline reference and emergency-triage app is for hikers, backpackers, hunters, search-and-rescue volunteers, and outdoor educators. The app has six field calculators (water purification, shelter selection, daylight estimation, pace and distance, wind chill and heat index, triage priority), 63+ reference entries (shelters, fire, water, navigation, knots, plants, wildlife, signals, first aid), and four adaptive color themes, with a tactical-red mode that preserves night vision. It includes the US Army Survival Manual (FM 21-76), SAS Survival Handbook, CDC guidelines, and NOAA solar algorithms.

Free. No accounts, no tracking, no advertising, no data collection. Works in airplane mode.

Burn Wizard

Pediatric burn assessment and fluid resuscitation, taught offline.

This educational app teaches the Lund-Browder method for total body surface area (TBSA) calculation, the Parkland formula for fluid resuscitation, and the Holliday-Segar 4-2-1 method for maintenance fluids. It includes an interactive body diagram with age-adjusted percentages, an 8-chapter curriculum from pathophysiology to long-term management, clinical documentation templates, and full English / Latin-American Spanish translation. It is built for medical students, nursing students, physician assistants, surgical residents, emergency medicine fellows, and first responders preparing for clinical practice.

Free. Works fully offline. No accounts, no analytics, no data collection. Local storage is encrypted with AES-256-GCM. Educational tool only — not a clinical device and not for direct patient care.

BoardBound

AI Tutor for FNP Board Exams.

An AI-powered study companion helps nurse practitioner students prepare for AANP and ANCC FNP board certification. A live AI tutor explains clinical reasoning like a real NP preceptor would. It stays within the stored rationale attached to each question, so it explains that reasoning instead of creating clinical facts of its own. Questions and rationales are AI-generated and machine-audited. They have not been reviewed by a licensed clinician. It includes a 500+ question bank tagged to both exam blueprints, spaced repetition, Deep Dive mode for complex multi-system reasoning, and a readiness dashboard. A free tier is included.

Pricing: $29.99 per month or $79.99 for a 3-month sprint.

Shortless

Short-form video feed blocker for Safari.

Shortless removes the infinite-scroll short-form feeds (YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, TikTok, and Snapchat Spotlight) out of Safari on iPhone and iPad, and leaves the rest of each site working normally: regular YouTube videos, Instagram photos, and Snapchat messages stay. There is a toggle for each platform. A Time Reclaimed dashboard, a scroll-free streak, and home and lock screen widgets show what you have taken back. It works in Safari only. iOS does not let content blockers change other browsers or native apps.

Free. No accounts, no analytics, no servers, no network requests. Everything stays on your device.

Scrollometer

Your screen time, measured as a distance.

Scrollometer turns the time you spend in the apps you pick into how far you've scrolled: feet, then miles, then landmarks. You can see today's distance, your history, and a weekly recap you can share if you want to. A Methodology screen shows the exact math behind every number. The numbers are estimates, and the app says so wherever it shows one: iOS reports time spent, not scrolling, so Scrollometer multiplies that time by the height of your screen and typical scroll speed for each app. There are no streaks, no badges, or leaderboards. You need iOS 17 and Screen Time access.

Free. Contains no networking code: no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising.

About

Martin Apps LLC is an Alabama limited liability company with one founder. For two audiences we know well, we build small, focused mobile and reference applications: healthcare learners and clinicians who need accurate teaching tools, and outdoor users operating in environments where connectivity is unreliable and decisions have consequences.

Our products aren't for general use. They are narrow, deep, and meet standards that fit the context: clinical accuracy in healthcare education; sourced and citeable content in the outdoor reference category; offline-first design wherever a user might lose their connection when they need the app most.

We also make two small screen-time tools, Shortless and Scrollometer, for people who want some of their attention back. Like everything else here, they keep your data on your device.

We do not build attention-economy products. Our apps do not contain advertising, do not sell user data, and do not optimize for engagement metrics that are uncorrelated with whether the user actually got the answer they needed.

How we work

Three commitments distinguish Martin Apps products from general-purpose alternatives in the same categories:

Clinician- or practitioner-designed

Every product is led by someone with active domain experience. BoardBound and Burn Wizard are built by a DNP-prepared nurse practitioner with a clinical background in pediatric critical care and pediatric neuro-oncology. Wilderness Survival Companion was sourced and structured against authoritative survival doctrine, not crowd-sourced advice.

Sources are explicit

BoardBound's AI tutor bases every clinical explanation on the rationale stored with the question. The model is told to refuse rather than make things up when the rationale doesn't support a claim. AI generates the questions and rationales and they are machine-audited. A licensed clinician hasn't reviewed them. Burn Wizard's calculations (Lund-Browder, Parkland, Holliday-Segar) are established clinical formulas. Their methods are explained transparently in-app. Every field tool and reference page in Wilderness Survival Companion traces back to a citable source (FM 21-76, SAS, CDC, NOAA).

Built to work when the network can't

Burn Wizard and Wilderness Survival Companion run fully offline. BoardBound stores its question bank locally so review sessions don't stall on a flaky cell signal at the library or in the hospital basement. Across the portfolio, connectivity-resilient design is a design constraint, not an afterthought.

Standards and validation

We treat content accuracy as a product feature, not a marketing line. Concrete examples:

  • BoardBound ships with an internal evaluation harness ("BoardBound Bench") that grades the AI tutor against a 100-case golden set on every change to the production prompt. Refusal canaries (PHI handling, jailbreak resistance) must pass at 100% or the change is blocked. Fidelity must clear a per-category floor or the change is blocked. The harness runs against a production-mirror of the prompt and uses an LLM-as-judge with rationale-aware grading; we publish the per-category pass rate in our internal release notes for every prompt iteration.
  • Burn Wizard teaches only well-established clinical methods (Lund-Browder, Parkland, Holliday-Segar) and displays an unambiguous educational-only disclaimer at app launch, on the assessment screen, and on every calculation result. The app is filed under Apple's Education primary category (Medical secondary) precisely because it teaches; it is not a clinical decision-support device and does not market itself as one.
  • Wilderness Survival Companion sources every reference entry from primary survival doctrine and tags each with the source. Edible-plant entries include explicit toxic-lookalike warnings rather than encouraging field identification by visual similarity alone. Calculators use established NOAA / CDC / National Weather Service algorithms.
  • Across the portfolio, we include explicit non-medical-advice and non-affiliation language where applicable. We are not affiliated with AANPCB® or ANCC® (BoardBound), with any burn center, professional society, or regulatory body (Burn Wizard), or with any government, military, or rescue agency (Wilderness Survival Companion).
