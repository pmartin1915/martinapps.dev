About

Martin Apps LLC is a single-founder Alabama limited liability company. We build small, focused mobile and reference applications for two audiences we know well: healthcare learners and clinicians who need accurate teaching tools, and outdoor users operating in environments where connectivity is unreliable and decisions have consequences.

Our products are not general-purpose. They are narrow, deep, and held to standards appropriate for the context: clinical accuracy in healthcare education; sourced and citeable content in the outdoor reference category; offline-first design wherever a user might be cut off from the network when they need the app most.

We do not build attention-economy products. Our apps do not contain advertising, do not sell user data, and do not optimize for engagement metrics that are uncorrelated with whether the user actually got the answer they needed.

Wilderness Survival Companion

Offline reference and field tools for the backcountry.

A 100% offline reference and emergency-triage app for hikers, backpackers, hunters, search-and-rescue volunteers, and outdoor educators. Six field calculators (water purification, shelter selection, daylight estimation, pace and distance, wind chill and heat index, triage priority), 63+ reference entries (shelters, fire, water, navigation, knots, plants, wildlife, signals, first aid), and four adaptive color themes including a tactical-red mode that preserves night vision. Sources include the US Army Survival Manual (FM 21-76), SAS Survival Handbook, CDC guidelines, and NOAA solar algorithms.

Free. No accounts, no tracking, no advertising, no data collection. Works in airplane mode.

Burn Wizard

Pediatric burn assessment and fluid resuscitation, taught offline.

An educational app that teaches the Lund-Browder method for total body surface area (TBSA) calculation, the Parkland formula for fluid resuscitation, and the Holliday-Segar 4-2-1 method for maintenance fluids. Includes an interactive body diagram with age-adjusted percentages, an 8-chapter curriculum spanning pathophysiology through long-term management, clinical documentation templates, and full English / Latin-American Spanish translation. Built for medical students, nursing students, physician assistants, surgical residents, emergency medicine fellows, and first responders preparing for clinical practice.

Free. Works fully offline. No accounts, no analytics, no data collection. Local storage is encrypted with AES-256-GCM. Educational tool only — not a clinical device and not for direct patient care.

BoardBound

AI Tutor for FNP Board Exams.

An AI-powered study companion for nurse practitioner students preparing for AANP and ANCC FNP board certification. A live AI tutor explains clinical reasoning the way a real NP preceptor would. It is constrained to the stored rationale attached to each question, so it explains that reasoning rather than generating clinical facts of its own. Questions and rationales are AI-generated and machine-audited; they have not been reviewed by a licensed clinician. Includes a 500+ question bank tagged to both exam blueprints, spaced repetition, Deep Dive mode for complex multi-system reasoning, and a readiness dashboard. Free tier included.

Pricing: $29.99 per month or $79.99 for a 3-month sprint.

How we work

Three commitments distinguish Martin Apps products from general-purpose alternatives in the same categories:

Clinician- or practitioner-designed

Every product is led by someone with active domain experience. BoardBound and Burn Wizard are built by an active DNP-FNP candidate with a clinical background in pediatric critical care and pediatric neuro-oncology. Wilderness Survival Companion was sourced and structured against authoritative survival doctrine, not crowd-sourced advice.

Sources are explicit

For BoardBound's AI tutor, every clinical explanation is grounded in the stored rationale attached to the question; the model is prompted to refuse rather than fabricate when the rationale doesn't support a claim. Questions and rationales are AI-generated and machine-audited; they have not been reviewed by a licensed clinician. Burn Wizard's calculations (Lund-Browder, Parkland, Holliday-Segar) are established clinical formulas with their methodology explained transparently in-app. Wilderness Survival Companion traces every field tool and reference page back to a citable source (FM 21-76, SAS, CDC, NOAA).

Built to work when the network can't

Burn Wizard and Wilderness Survival Companion are fully offline. BoardBound caches its question bank locally so review sessions don't stall on a flaky cell signal at the library or in the hospital basement. Connectivity-resilient design is a design constraint across the portfolio, not an afterthought.

Standards and validation

We treat content accuracy as a product feature, not a marketing line. Concrete examples:

  • BoardBound ships with an internal evaluation harness ("BoardBound Bench") that grades the AI tutor against a 100-case golden set on every change to the production prompt. Refusal canaries (PHI handling, jailbreak resistance) must pass at 100% or the change is blocked. Fidelity must clear a per-category floor or the change is blocked. The harness runs against a production-mirror of the prompt and uses an LLM-as-judge with rationale-aware grading; we publish the per-category pass rate in our internal release notes for every prompt iteration.
  • Burn Wizard teaches only well-established clinical methods (Lund-Browder, Parkland, Holliday-Segar) and displays an unambiguous educational-only disclaimer at app launch, on the assessment screen, and on every calculation result. The app is filed under Apple's Education primary category (Medical secondary) precisely because it teaches; it is not a clinical decision-support device and does not market itself as one.
  • Wilderness Survival Companion sources every reference entry from primary survival doctrine and tags each with the source. Edible-plant entries include explicit toxic-lookalike warnings rather than encouraging field identification by visual similarity alone. Calculators use established NOAA / CDC / National Weather Service algorithms.
  • Across the portfolio, we include explicit non-medical-advice and non-affiliation language where applicable. We are not affiliated with AANPCB® or ANCC® (BoardBound), with any burn center, professional society, or regulatory body (Burn Wizard), or with any government, military, or rescue agency (Wilderness Survival Companion).
