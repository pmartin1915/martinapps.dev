Wilderness Survival Companion

Survival know-how and field tools for hikers, hunters and rescue volunteers. It works with no signal, and every entry names its source.

Get help

iPhone and Android · Free · No account, no tracking, no ads

See it in action

What it does

Field tools

A compass with saved locations, a flashlight that flashes SOS, water treatment amounts, daylight times and a guide to emergency priorities.

A reference you can check

128+ entries on first aid, plants, wildlife, knots, shelter and fire. Each comes from a named source such as the Army survival manual, NOLS or the CDC.

Readable in sun or dark

Four color modes, including a red mode for use in the dark.

Before you download

  • A reference, not a replacement for wilderness training or emergency services. Call 911 when you can.
  • Phone GPS can be off. Carry a paper map and compass.
  • Works fully offline. No account, no tracking, no ads.
