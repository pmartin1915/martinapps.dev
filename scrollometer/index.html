Scrollometer

See your screen time as a distance: feet, then miles, then landmarks.

Get help

iPhone · Free · Needs iOS 17 and Screen Time access

See it in action

What it does

Today

Your estimated distance so far, and the landmark you've passed.

Your record

How your recent weeks went, and a weekly recap you can share if you want to.

It shows its math

The Methodology screen gives the exact arithmetic and where each figure comes from.

Before you download

  • The numbers are estimates. iOS reports time, not scrolling, so the app multiplies time by typical scroll speed and screen height.
  • No streaks, badges or leaderboards, on purpose.
  • No networking code at all: no account, no server, no analytics, no ads.
