Burn Wizard

Learn how burn size and fluid needs are worked out for children and adults, step by step, with no internet needed.

Get help

iPhone · Free · English and Spanish

See it in action

What it does

Practice sizing a burn on a body map

Tap the burned areas. The Lund-Browder chart adjusts each body part's share for age.

Practice the fluid math

Work through the Parkland formula and the 4-2-1 maintenance method, with every step shown.

Learn the whole picture

An 8-chapter course and practice cases, from how burns injure the body to recovery.

Before you download

  • For learning only. It is not a medical device and not for diagnosis or patient care.
  • The cases are for practice. Never use its results to make decisions about a real patient.
  • No account and no data collection. What you enter stays on your phone, encrypted.
