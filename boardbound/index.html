BoardBound

AI Tutor for FNP Board Exams

BoardBound is an AI-powered study companion for nurse practitioner students preparing for Family Nurse Practitioner (FNP) board certification — built by a DNP-prepared nurse practitioner who trained in the same family nurse practitioner track.

Traditional question banks show you a correct answer and a paragraph of text. BoardBound adds a live AI tutor trained to explain clinical reasoning the way a real NP preceptor would — walking you through why the right answer is right, where your thinking went off, and how to connect it to evidence-based guidelines.

What you get

Free tier

  • 30 practice questions per day
  • 3 AI tutor conversations per day
  • Core performance tracking by domain
  • Access to both AANP and ANCC blueprints

Paid tier — $29.99/month or $79.99 for a 3-month sprint

  • Full 500+ question bank, tagged to AANP and ANCC blueprints
  • Unlimited AI tutor conversations (with a monthly fair-use cap)
  • Deep Dive mode (5 per day) for complex multi-system clinical reasoning
  • Spaced repetition scheduler so weak topics surface when you need them
  • Full progress dashboard — readiness score, domain breakdown, study streaks

Built for boards

Every question is tagged to the official exam blueprint you choose:

  • AANP FNP — Assess, Diagnose, Plan, Evaluate domains
  • ANCC FNP-BC — Assessment, Diagnosis, Planning, Implementation, Evaluation domains

Questions span the full lifespan: pediatrics, adolescents, adults, older adults, and prenatal/perinatal — in the distribution your exam actually uses.

AI tutor — designed for safety

The AI tutor is constrained to the fixed rationale stored with each question. It explains that rationale — it does not generate clinical facts of its own, and it does not answer from open-ended general knowledge. It does not replace your clinical judgment, your preceptor, or evidence-based practice guidelines.

Questions and rationales are AI-generated and machine-audited; they have not been reviewed by a licensed clinician.

Every AI response includes a static educational-use disclaimer, and every response has a one-tap "Flag for Clinical Inaccuracy" button. Flagged responses are reviewed and used to improve the tutor.

Privacy first

  • AI tutor access is opt-in. You see a clear consent screen before your first conversation, naming the AI provider.
  • Never enter personal health information (PHI) into the tutor — it is for exam reasoning only.
  • Your questions and AI conversations are stored securely and tied only to your account.
  • You can delete your account and all associated data from Settings at any time.
Important disclaimer. BoardBound is an educational exam-preparation tool. It is not medical advice and must never be used for patient care decisions.
