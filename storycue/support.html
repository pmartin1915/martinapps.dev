StoryCue Support

StoryCue · Guided interviews, recorded on your iPhone

StoryCue shows one question at a time from a question deck and records each answer with your iPhone's camera and microphone, so you can capture the stories of the people you love.

Need help? Email us at support@martinapps.dev and we'll respond within one business day.

Getting Started

  • Choose a question deck.
  • Read the consent card aloud, so everyone on camera agrees to be recorded.
  • Allow camera and microphone access when asked.
  • Tap record, ask the question on screen, and move to the next question when the answer is done.
  • Find your sessions under Recordings, where you can play, export, or delete them.

Frequently Asked Questions

The camera or microphone doesn't work.

Open Settings → StoryCue and turn on Camera and Microphone, then return to StoryCue.

Where are my recordings stored?

Only on your iPhone, inside StoryCue. They are included in your iPhone's own iCloud or computer backup if you have one turned on. StoryCue has no account and no servers, and makes no network requests.

How do I keep a recording somewhere else?

Open the session under Recordings and tap Export. You can save it as one video or one video per answer, either to Photos or, with Save or share…, to Files or another app.

The app closed while I was recording. Did I lose the answer?

Usually not. The next time you open StoryCue, it looks for recordings that were interrupted and offers them to you to keep or discard.

My iPhone is almost full.

StoryCue warns you when free space is low, because long answers may not fit. Export the sessions you want to keep, then delete them in StoryCue to free space.
